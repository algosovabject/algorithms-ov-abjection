## VOCABULARY

### Ghosting
Layering reversed/slowed voice against itself to create temporal self-haunting.

### Beating
Two near frequencies creating emergent pulse instability.

### Shredding
Granular fragmentation of vocal identity into randomized debris.

### Silence Wells
Intentional dead-space long enough to destabilize listener expectation.

### Machine Breathing
Slow cyclic bass modulation resembling industrial respiration.
