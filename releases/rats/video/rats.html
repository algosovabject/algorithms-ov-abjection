Rat Proliferation Simulator

SUPER_RATS.PY is a project designed to simulate the evolution of rats through a genetic algorithm. The goal is to evolve generations of rats to reach an ideal weight of 50000 grams, which is considered optimal for human entertainment purposes.

Simulation

  • Generations of rats are forced to evolve through natural selection.
  • Breeding pairs are selected based on their fitness in the environment.
  • The unfit individuals are culled from the population.
  • The process is repeated over multiple generations until ideal weight is achieved.
  • A bifurcated audio/visual signal is generated to indicate the evolution of the rat population.
  • A bifurcated audio/visual signal is generated to indicate the mental state of the rat population.

Generation

  • 50000 grams is the ideal weight for rats in this simulation.
  • Rats that reach this weight are considered to have achieved optimal health and fitness.
  • 50000 grams is the average weight of a medium-sized dog.
  • Rats are not naturally meant to weigh 50000 grams.
  • Rats that reach this weight are considered to be in peak condition.
